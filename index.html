What it answers

Four questions, one set of numbers.

Most tools show you what happened. 1271 is built around what you need to decide next, and it says plainly when something is still unknown instead of pretending it is zero.

Cash

What can we afford, and when does it get tight?

Jobs

Which jobs are making money, and which one needs a decision?

Next

What should we do this week that changes the next quarter?

Honesty

What does the forecast not know yet, and who can answer it?

In the app today

Built on your books, not beside them.

Everything marked Live is in the app today. Items marked Coming soon are on the way.

Live

Your books, as they are

See your QuickBooks numbers, kept up to date, without changing how you keep them.

Connect QuickBooks Online, read-only and approved by Intuit, and your chart, ledger and invoices refresh on a schedule. Or upload the Account List and General Ledger exports. 1271 keeps the original evidence, checks its results against your reports and shows every transaction behind a number.

Live

Houzz Pro

Your leads and jobs from Houzz Pro show up in 1271 on their own.

Leads, projects and schedules read from Houzz Pro into the same pipeline and forecast. Nothing is written back.

Live

13-week cash and 12-month forecast

See the cash you'll have each week for the next three months, and how the year looks.

Weekly cash against your reserve, a twelve-month operating view, and committed, expected and upside views, plus your own what-ifs. Every figure carries its as-of date and says when inputs are incomplete.

Live

Cash floating in your jobs

See how much of your own cash is floating each job until you bill it, and which jobs look profitable on paper but haven't brought the cash in.

Revenue counts when you invoice. Job costs wait as work in progress until they're billed, and each job shows whether billing is ahead of or behind the work.

Live

Jobs and pipeline

See which jobs make money, and which bids are worth counting on.

Signed work, work left on signed jobs, budgets, projected final margin, and bids counted by how likely you are to win them. Converted bids are never counted twice.

Live

Crew hours and payroll

See where your crew's hours went and what you still owe them.

Import QuickBooks Time, match job codes and people, and see unassigned hours, overtime, bonuses and wages owed. Pay details stay restricted to the people allowed to see them.

Live

Open Items for your team

Ask your crew a quick question and get the answer on their phone.

"Which job was this for?" goes to the person who knows, on their phone, with no financials attached. Their answer updates the forecast.

Live

CFO Brief and Data Health

A short read of what changed, and a list of what's missing.

A plain-language brief of what changed and what to review, generated from the numbers, not by an AI. Data Health lists what is missing, stale or unassigned, with the fix beside it.

Who it's for

Owners who run the work and want the numbers to keep up.

Remodelers, carpenters, tree care, concrete, flooring, handyman companies: businesses with crews, payroll, equipment and signed work ahead, where the books are in QuickBooks and the real information is in the field.

  • You do not need to understand journal entries, WIP or allocation policy. 1271 asks practical questions and shows its work.
  • Your accountant and bookkeeper stay. 1271 reads your books; it never changes them.
  • An advisor is optional. Founder Marcus Roesler still works with a small number of owners directly.

Unknown is not zero

A missing cost never produces a healthy-looking profit. You see the known subtotal and what is missing.

Every number has a source

As-of date, basis and the records behind it are one click away, on every screen.

Your data stays yours

Company-owned accounts, read-only connections, export on request. Pay details are restricted by role.

No black box

Forecasts are deterministic and reproducible. Snapshots are kept so you can compare what you expected with what happened.
